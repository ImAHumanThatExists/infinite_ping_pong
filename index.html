Move

Mouse/touch or W/S moves your paddle (outlined). ←/→ or Q/E picks who to fire at (never your own side); A/D (or the spot buttons) slides your aim spot along their goal, even to a corner. The ball goes via the middle, then to the spot: follow the dotted line. Goals show goals conceded (lower is better). In Last standing, one goal against you and you are out.
